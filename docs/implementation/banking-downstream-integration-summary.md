# Banking Downstream Integration Summary

## Purpose

This pass closes the gap between imported/reconciled bank transactions and the wider accounting workflows. The core rule is that bank imports remain operational records, while reconciled or created transactions update funds, accounts, reports, dashboards, Gift Aid, suppliers, payroll, and month-end readiness through posted ledger activity.

## Posting And Duplication Rules

- Imported statement rows create `bank_lines` only.
- Reconciliation actions create or confirm one source record for each bank line.
- Manual transactions are posted through `postManualTransactionToLedger`.
- Donations created from bank lines use the donation reconciliation flow.
- Gift Aid claim receipts update the submitted claim batch payment reconciliation fields.
- Confirmed reconciliation matches are protected by database-level partial unique indexes so one bank transaction and one matched source cannot be confirmed twice.
- Reports count posted ledger activity once, not both raw bank rows and generated journals.
- The expected outcome is no duplicate ledger posting from matching, creating, splitting, donation reconciliation, or Gift Aid claim payment reconciliation.

## Funds And Accounts

Fund history and account balances are updated through posted `journal_lines`. Reconciled bank activity therefore appears in fund and account reporting when the source transaction has been posted to the ledger.

The reconciliation actions invalidate organisation report cache after posting or donation reconciliation so downstream fund/account/report views are refreshed.

## Transactions

Manual transactions matched to bank transactions are marked `matched`, linked to the bank transaction, reconciled, and posted once. Duplicate posting is prevented by existing source availability checks, existing bank match checks, and the new confirmed-match unique indexes.

## Donations And Gift Aid

Donation reconciliation creates or links a donation record from an incoming bank line. The donation path links the donor where selected, updates donation history, and hands off to Gift Aid assessment/eligibility through the existing Gift Aid reconciliation model.

HMRC Gift Aid claim receipts can be matched to submitted claim batches. When confirmed, the claim batch stores the received bank transaction, received payment total, reconciled payment status, and payment reconciliation timestamp.

## Suppliers, Invoices, And Payroll

Supplier and payroll matching continue to flow through source records and posted journals rather than raw bank rows. Bank rules and match suggestions provide categorisation and matching hints; confirmed matches update the bank line status and source linkage without duplicating ledger entries.

## Reports And Dashboard

Reports should read posted ledger activity once:

- Income & Expenditure
- Balance Sheet
- Fund Movement
- Fund Balances
- Account Activity
- Cash Summary
- Bank Reconciliation Report
- Trustee Summary

Dashboard banking metrics now include:

- total bank cash from latest statement balances
- unreconciled transactions
- stale bank imports
- balance differences
- month-end readiness

Month-end readiness is false when there are unreconciled bank lines, balance differences, stale imports, or statement continuity warnings.

## Reconciliation Certificate

Treasurers can generate a reconciliation certificate for a bank account statement import. The certificate snapshot stores:

- bank account
- statement period
- closing bank balance
- book balance
- difference
- reconciled transaction count
- unreconciled exceptions
- generated by
- generated at

A PDF export endpoint is available for trustee/internal records.

## Statement Gap Detection

Import preview and import completion detect statement continuity issues:

- date overlap with the previous statement
- date gap after the previous statement
- prior closing balance mismatch against current opening balance

Warnings are stored on `bank_statement_imports` and surfaced in the Banking statement history.

## Remaining Improvements

- Add richer supplier invoice/payment-run and payroll-run source adapters to the matching engine where domain-specific payment status fields are available.
- Promote reconciliation certificates into a dedicated certificate detail page with trustee sign-off metadata.
- Precompute top suggested matches for large transaction lists to avoid per-row suggestion calls in table views.
